# UAT Rules Inventory — IAM Automation Phase 1

## Approved product boundary

Phase 1 is a **read-only identity reconciliation and audit platform**. It ingests approved exports, normalizes records, matches identities, builds an internal canonical analytical employee/worker representation, classifies findings, preserves evidence/history, supports human review, and produces reports/evidence exports.

Phase 1 may mutate internal analytical/runtime state required for ingestion, reconciliation, lineage, review state, evidence, local notifications, backups, and technical snapshot distribution. It must not make authoritative IAM decisions or mutate authoritative source platforms. Humans decide; authoritative platforms execute.

Scope classifications:

- **PHASE1_CORE** — required to prove reconciliation, finding classification, evidence, review, and exports.
- **PHASE1_PLATFORM** — technical capability required to operate Phase 1 safely.
- **PHASE2_WRITER** — performs, approves, applies, or orchestrates authoritative business changes.
- **FUTURE_INTEGRATION** — future connector or downstream capability, not required for Phase 1 acceptance.
- **OUT_OF_SCOPE** — repository behavior not part of current acceptance.

Markers:

- **BUSINESS DECISION REQUIRED** — expected finding interpretation or policy is not approved.
- **ASSUMPTION CURRENTLY ENCODED** — code selects an outcome without clear business approval.
- **CONTRADICTION** — repository sources disagree; this does not expand Phase 1 scope.

## Phase 1 finding classification model

For each matched field, population comparison, lifecycle observation, or source absence, the analytical platform should classify evidence—not choose the business outcome:

| Finding class | Meaning for UAT |
|---|---|
| `CONSISTENT` | Observations agree within the approved comparison rules. |
| `EXPECTED_DIFFERENCE` | Difference is known and valid for the population/process. |
| `SOURCE_LATENCY` | Difference may be explained by an approved refresh/arrival window. |
| `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` | Available data cannot safely support a conclusion. |
| `DISCREPANCY` | Evidence differs beyond the accepted rule/window and needs human review. |
| `POLICY_RELEVANT_FINDING` | Evidence may require a human business decision; Phase 1 does not make it. |
